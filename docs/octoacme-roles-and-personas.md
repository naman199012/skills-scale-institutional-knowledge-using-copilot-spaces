# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists ensure product quality, reliability, and performance through systematic testing and acceptance validation. They collaborate with developers and product managers to identify defects early and validate that solutions meet requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Create and execute comprehensive test plans
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Validate acceptance criteria before accepting work as complete
- Perform regression testing and smoke tests
- Contribute to test automation strategy and maintain automated test suites
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test coverage

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and quality risks
- Provide early feedback on issues and edge cases
- Enable fast, confident deployments through rigorous validation
- Improve test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with developers and product managers
- Defect triage in weekly syncs
- QA metrics and test coverage reporting
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria definition

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, guide architectural decisions, and mentor developers on design and best practices. They balance innovation with maintainability and help the team navigate complex technical challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide technical design decisions
- Identify architectural risks and propose solutions
- Mentor developers on best practices and code quality
- Advocate for technical excellence and system reliability
- Help estimate technical complexity and feasibility
- Monitor technical debt and propose refactoring initiatives

### Goals
- Maintain system reliability, scalability, and security
- Reduce technical debt and improve code maintainability
- Enable team growth through mentoring and knowledge sharing
- Ensure architecture aligns with long-term product vision

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- One-on-ones and mentoring sessions with developers
- Technical risk escalation to Project and Product Managers
- Architecture documentation and decision logs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and enable continuous improvement through Agile practices. They coach the team on Agile principles and help optimize delivery rhythm and team dynamics.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- Identify and help resolve blockers and impediments
- Track team velocity and suggest process improvements
- Coach team members and Project Manager on Agile practices
- Monitor team capacity and workload distribution
- Foster psychological safety and open communication

### Goals
- Optimize team productivity and delivery predictability
- Maintain healthy team dynamics and engagement
- Reduce process friction and administrative overhead
- Enable continuous improvement through regular feedback loops

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Sprint planning and retrospective facilitation
- One-on-ones with team members to understand blockers
- Metrics reporting on team velocity and cycle time

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical implementation by analyzing stakeholder needs, documenting detailed requirements, and validating solutions. They ensure clarity and traceability from business objectives to delivered functionality.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Create detailed acceptance criteria and user stories
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Identify risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Maintain traceability between requirements and implementation
- Facilitate conversations between business stakeholders and technical teams

### Goals
- Ensure solutions align with business needs and objectives
- Reduce rework due to misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Maintain clear traceability and documentation
- Enable effective collaboration between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria reviews and refinement
- Sign-off documentation and validation reports
- Cross-team coordination on dependencies and scope

---

## UX / Design Professional

### Role Summary
UX/Design Professionals design user experiences and interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs and product strategy. They advocate for the user throughout the product development process.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Define design systems and maintain design consistency
- Collaborate on accessibility compliance (WCAG, ARIA)
- Review implementations to ensure design fidelity
- Iterate designs based on user feedback and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered solutions that solve real problems
- Improve usability, adoption, and user satisfaction
- Maintain design consistency across the product
- Ensure accessibility for all users

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design system documentation and guidelines
- Accessibility audits and recommendations

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, and remove organizational blockers to ensure project success. They represent executive leadership and ensure projects align with strategic objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and strategic priorities
- Secure budget, staffing, and other resources
- Resolve escalated issues and organizational blockers
- Provide executive oversight and governance
- Approve major decisions and scope changes
- Communicate project importance and status to leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Remove organizational barriers to success
- Maintain alignment with strategic direction
- Enable teams to operate effectively with necessary resources

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and executive briefings
- Approval gates and decision checkpoints
- Escalation resolution and blocker removal
- Strategic planning and portfolio-level alignment

---

## How These Personas Interact

### Cross-functional Collaboration
- **QA & Developers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria, test coverage, and testability during implementation
- **Technical Lead & Project Manager**: Technical Lead advises PM on feasibility, technical risks, and complexity estimates
- **Business Analyst & Developers**: BA clarifies requirements and acceptance criteria; Developers implement and provide feasibility feedback
- **UX & Product Manager**: UX researches and designs solutions; PM validates fit with business objectives
- **UX & Developers**: UX provides design specifications; Developers implement and provide technical feedback
- **Scrum Master & Project Manager**: SM facilitates day-to-day execution; PM handles stakeholder communication and planning
- **Sponsor & Project/Product Managers**: Sponsor provides strategic guidance and removes blockers; PM/PdM report progress and escalate decisions

### Escalation Paths
- Individual contributors escalate through their functional leads (Technical Lead for developers, Scrum Master for process issues)
- Teams escalate to Project Manager and Product Manager
- Cross-team or business-impacting issues escalate to Sponsor with Project Manager mediation
